How to play

  1. Pass the phone around. Each player drags their card up to see the secret word.
  2. The imposter doesn't get the word. With hints on, they get a loose clue instead. With more than one imposter, each gets a different clue and sees who their teammates are.
  3. Going round the circle, everyone says one word linked to the secret word. Be specific enough to prove you know it, vague enough not to give it away.
  4. The imposter bluffs and tries to work out the word from what everyone else says.
  5. After a couple of rounds, vote on who the imposter is, then tap Reveal Imposter.
  6. Then each imposter gets one guess at the word. Imposters score for getting away and for guessing the word, and everyone else scores for each imposter caught.
